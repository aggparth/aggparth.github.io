About

I'm a master's student in the Department of Biosciences and Bioengineering at IIT Roorkee, working in the Singla Lab with Prof. Jitin Singla. I came to computation from a biotechnology degree, and my work sits between the two.

My main interests are cryo-EM and cryo-ET, deep learning for structural biology, and implicit neural representations, where a neural network itself stores a 3D density map. I'm particularly drawn to using them for heterogeneous reconstruction, alignment and pose estimation.

Research

  • Reference-free subtomogram alignment

    M.Tech thesis · Singla Lab · June 2026 – present

    In cryo-electron tomography, copies of a molecule are cut out of a 3D reconstruction of the cell as small subvolumes (subtomograms). Each copy sits at an unknown orientation, and part of its information is missing because of how the sample is tilted in the microscope (the "missing wedge"). I'm developing a method that aligns all the particles together, with no starting template, by gradient ascent on SFSC, a spectral signal-to-noise score that accounts for the missing wedge.

  • Deep learning methods in cryo-EM and cryo-ET: a survey first author

    Aggarwal, P., Singla, J. · in preparation

    A comprehensive survey of deep learning across the cryo-EM and cryo-ET pipelines, including implicit neural representation architectures and activation functions and the CryoDRGN line of methods for heterogeneous reconstruction.

  • Review of AI and nanotechnology for microbiome engineering

    B.Tech thesis · DTU · Aug 2024 – May 2025 · supervised by Dr. Prakash Chandra

    A survey of machine learning and nanotechnology in microbiome engineering, from predictive models of microbial interactions to nanodevice-based interventions.

Projects

cryoET-Heterogeneity↗

Python package · Sep 2026

An analysis layer on top of CryoDRGN-ET latent spaces: clustering across K with three quality metrics, representative states taken as real particles rather than averages, trajectory analysis and null-model tests. On a real dataset, the latent space shows clear structure compared with a matched random baseline, but no single number of states is supported: four criteria give four answers.

4,549particles analysed
26.5–77.1σabove the null
